Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Welcome to SketchDraw ("we", "our", or "us"). SketchDraw is a free digital drawing web application accessible at https://sketchdraw-one.vercel.app/. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver our drawing experience, authenticate your account, and maintain your personal drawing streak:

  • Google Account Information (OAuth): When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive basic profile details permitted by you via Google OAuth: your email address, your display name, and your profile avatar URL.
  • Drawing & Streak Data: We store your daily drawing streak count, highest streak records, earned badges, and saved drawing preferences.
  • Artwork & Canvases: Your drawing strokes and artwork are rendered and stored locally in your browser storage. We do not inspect, claim ownership of, or sell your original artwork.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate and verify your identity when signing in.
  • Display your username, profile picture, and drawing streak in the SketchDraw app and on the community streak leaderboard.
  • Save your active drawing preferences (surface physics, tool settings, and favorite brushes).
  • Provide technical support and app notifications if requested.

3. Google User Data Policy & Compliance

SketchDraw's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not use your Google user data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not sell your personal or Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers under any circumstances.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, except as strictly necessary to provide the service (such as database storage on Supabase).
  • No humans read your personal data unless required for security investigations or with your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage & Security

Authentication and streak data are securely handled via our backend infrastructure on Supabase using industry-standard encrypted PostgreSQL databases with Row Level Security (RLS) and HTTPS encryption in transit.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You retain full control over your personal data at all times. You can request deletion of your account, drawing history, and all associated personal data by emailing us directly at jvrghvn0@gmail.com. Upon receiving your request, your records will be permanently removed within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

SketchDraw is a general audience creative drawing application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate parental or guardian consent.

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or SketchDraw's privacy practices, please contact our developer team at:
